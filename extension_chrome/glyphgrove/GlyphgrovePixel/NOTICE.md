## Release review remains open

The exact subset and provenance of individual logo and Material Design icons has not been reconciled against their source assets; trademark rights and any third-party icon-specific permissions require review. The pinned Nerd inventory and Font Logos license file disagree in their description. The sufficiency of attribution and interaction of source licenses with a single combined OFL font remain unresolved. Do not assume a repository-level license grants trademark rights. The compiled code is available under the included MIT license; upstream font and icon license terms remain applicable independently.
